Help & support

Some guidance for submitting a useful complaint and following it up.

✳

What should I include?

Describe what happened, where it happened, when you noticed it, and how it affects the area. Clear details help a team understand the concern.

◎

How do I track it?

After submission, copy the reference ID shown in the confirmation message. Use the Track a Complaint page to view its latest status.

Track a complaint →
⌂

Where is it routed?

The current prototype suggests a department from the selected category. The mapping is rule-based and may need adjustment for a real local authority.
